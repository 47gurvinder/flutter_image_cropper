# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Constructive technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, discriminatory language, harassment, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not.

Contributors should:

- communicate with empathy and professional courtesy;
- critique ideas and code rather than people;
- respect differing experience levels and perspectives;
- accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and moderation;
- protect private or sensitive information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through [gurwinderdevx.com](https://gurwinderdevx.com). Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential to the extent practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when behavior threatens the safety or health of the community. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

This policy applies in repository spaces and whenever someone publicly represents the project.
